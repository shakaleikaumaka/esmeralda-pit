The Knowledge Transponder

Press play. The words glow.

Every session opens the same way 🌊 — a tiny window plays the talk in the corner, small on purpose (some days our village wifi buckled under 800 builders, and small windows forgive small pictures), the Esmeralda sunset breathes behind it, and below, the transcript glows paragraph by paragraph as it's spoken — calm, no chasing. Tap any paragraph to jump there. Want the big picture? Expand the window — with love and aloha, forgive a little latency. The words carry everything.

🕳️ Why the P.I.T.?

Public Information Transmission

P.I.T. is the Knowledge Transponder's true backronym — and it's named twice over. For the orchestra pit, where the musicians sit: the ones who make the sound the whole room dances to, rarely in the spotlight, always holding the signal. And in honor of OSO P.I.T. — Shaka's ETHGlobal Delhi hackathon winner (Most Creative Use of ENS), where the pit first got its name.

publicinform.com 🕳️✨

And the pit is only just beginning — onward, sharing this openly with communities everywhere, for all: the open transponding of knowledge. The pit provides! 💪🕳️

This archive is a gift. 🌺

For a month, 800+ of us built a village in Sonoma County — and inside The Loft, the talks never stopped. I had the honor of running the A/V and stewarding the vibe of that room. This is what we captured together.

Everything here is open. Watch it, read it, quote it, teach with it, remix it into your own popup city. The knowledge belongs to the village — open source the knowledge, not just the music.

If this archive sparks something for you, pass it on. That's the whole economy.

And if you want to build with it — the whole pit is on GitHub: this site, the data, the transcripts, and the full 20-script pipeline that harvested, transcribed, and published everything. Everything open-sourced CC0. Fork me like crazy. 🍴
